Common questions

Is this real-money gambling?
No. Every game uses play chips with no cash value. There are no prizes and nothing to buy.
How do I get a hint?
Tap Hint on your turn. The coach shows its move, your chance of winning, the chance you need to call, and what it thinks your opponents hold.
What do Good, OK and Mistake mean?
After each hand, every decision you made is compared with the coach. Good matches the coach, OK is a reasonable alternative, and Mistake cost you chips on average.
Can I practise a particular situation?
Yes. Choose Practise a hand on the home screen to pick your two cards, your seat, the number of players and the stack sizes.
How do I start over?
Open About & privacy from the home screen and tap Reset progress. Your table settings are kept.
Does the app collect my data?
No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking, and your progress stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
