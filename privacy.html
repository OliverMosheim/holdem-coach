Hold'em Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Hold'em Coach is a practice app for Texas Hold'em poker. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: the app does not collect any.

Information we collect

None. Hold'em Coach does not ask for, collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (chip count, statistics and table settings) is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with "Reset progress" in the app's About screen, or by deleting the app.

Internet access

The app works fully offline. It does not send any data over the internet.

Gambling

All games use play chips with no cash value. Hold'em Coach does not offer real-money gambling, prizes or purchases.

Children

Hold'em Coach is rated 17+ because it simulates gambling. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
